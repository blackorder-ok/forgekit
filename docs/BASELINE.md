## Feature preload

A full `/forge:feature` run spawns tester, developer, reviewer, security-reviewer, ops, ai-evaluator and doc-updater. Their combined preload is about 8.1k tokens. The test gate in `tests/test_forge.py` allows 8.2k, and the plan target is about 8k.

## Notes

- The 1.5k per-agent goal is not met by `security-reviewer` and `ops`. They carry more than one skill by design.
- Figures move with the skill digests. Re-run the check after editing any digest.
